# SOCIAL MEDIA USE ON TEENAGE MENTAL HEALTH AND LIFESTYLE

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

This study examines the effect that teen use of social media has on their mental well-being and lifestyle. The project will include a data-driven dashboard designed to assist in investigating the relationship among teen's mental health issues, lifestyle choices and the amount of time they spend on social media.

This study uses the DAR method (Dashboard, Analysis and Recommendations) to provide an interactive environment to analyze behavioral trends and risk factors for teens aged 13–19.

---

## PROBLEM STATEMENT

More and more teens are spending excessive amounts of time on social media. As much as we want to believe that this trend won't have an impact on their lives; it most certainly does. Excessive social media can contribute to:

- Sleep deprivation
- Increased stress levels
- Higher rates of anxiety
- Tendency towards addiction
- Depression risks
- Lower participation in physical activities
- Poorer grades in school

The goal of the dashboard is to reveal these relationships by allowing the viewer to interact with the visuals and test hypotheses.

---

## RESEARCH QUESTION

What is the relationship between a teenager’s use of social media and their mental health and lifestyle?

## HYPOTHESES

### H1
Students who spend more time on social media tend to get less sleep each day.

### H2
Increased social media usage tends to lead to greater levels of stress and anxiety.

### H3 
Students who have a higher tendency towards addiction are at a greater risk for developing depression.

### H4
Heavy social media users tend to perform poorly academically when compared to moderate users.

### H5 
A lack of physical activity, along with heavy screen exposure could be indicative of poor mental wellness.
